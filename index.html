What gets counted

  • IMG only. JPG, JPEG, and PNG attachments count. STK, VID, OPUS, and other files do not; an un-timestamped caption cannot hide the IMG line.
  • Same sender + same minute. Consecutive image records collapse to one fin, even across system lines.
  • Bare numbers are a check only. The highest manual tally never changes attribution or totals.
  • Phone matching is exact after cleanup. Saved display names stay visibly unmatched until you resolve them.
  • Daily means 08:00 → 08:00. Early-morning photos belong to the previous bucket.
